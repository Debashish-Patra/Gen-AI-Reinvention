## Two questions, two axes

Most confusion comes from mixing these up. Keep them apart, then read them together as a grid.

| Axis | The question | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Pillars** | What do we need to get right? | Five areas that must all be covered, so nothing important is missed. |

## The five pillars

*What to cover.* Each pillar answers one question. Skip one and the programme gets stuck later.

| # | Pillar | The question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lead with value** | Where is the money, and what comes first? |
| 2 | **Talent and new ways of working** | How do roles and skills change? |
| 3 | **Responsible AI** | What can go wrong, and where? |
| 4 | **Digital core** | What must be in place to run it at scale? |
| 5 | **Change and continuous reinvention** | How does it stick and keep evolving? |

> Pillars 1 to 4 build the programme. Pillar 5 keeps it alive after launch.

---

## The four phases

*When to do it.* Each phase ends at a gate. No gate passed, no scale-up. Timings are indicative.

| Phase | When | Goal | Gate to pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Frame** | Weeks 0 to 4 | Align on ambition and where value sits | Leaders agree where to play |
| **2. Prioritize** | Weeks 4 to 8 | Score the ideas and pick the right bets | Business case clears the hurdle |
| **3. Build and pilot** | Months 2 to 6 | Prove it small, with humans in the loop | Beats control, few risk incidents |
| **4. Scale and reinvent** | Month 6 onward | Industrialise and keep improving | Value tracked against the case |

## Marketing, in retail and travel

The structure is identical. Only the levers, risks and data change.

| | **Retail** (fashion retailer) | **Travel** (regional airline) |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | Cut content cost and lift repeat purchase | Cut campaign cost and lift loyalty engagement |
| **First pilot** | Product descriptions and email content for one category | Destination content and personalised offers for one market |
| **Bigger bet, later** | Next-best-offer, once customer data is unified | Next-best-action, once traveller data is unified |
| **Key risks** | Off-brand output, false price or promo claims, consent | Wrong fares or policies, cultural missteps, consent |
| **Digital core** | One customer profile, clean product catalogue, machine-readable brand guide | One traveller profile across booking, loyalty and service; destination content library |
| **Measured against** | A control group, on cost per asset and conversion | A control group, on cost per campaign and repeat bookings |

*Both companies are illustrative scenarios, not real client cases.*
